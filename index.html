HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is Mardan Digital Hub free to use?

Browsing the public directory is free. Advertising placements or JANJAMZ development services may have separate charges.

How can I add my shop or business?

Use the Add Business form. Your submission is reviewed before it appears publicly.

Are all business listings verified?

No. Listings show a verification status. Confirm contact details, hours and location with the business before travelling.

How can a service provider join?

Choose a provider registration option or contact JANJAMZ. Profiles are reviewed before publication.
